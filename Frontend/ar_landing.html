AR Payments

Record money received from customers and monitor payment history and outstanding invoice balances.

  • Record customer payments
  • View payment history
  • Track payment status
  • Review outstanding invoices
Open Payments →
Business workflow: A customer is registered first. An invoice is created for the goods or services sold. When the customer pays, the payment is recorded and the remaining balance is calculated.

Example: Invoice amount ₹75,000 − payment received ₹25,000 = outstanding balance ₹50,000.
